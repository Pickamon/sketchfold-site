Sketch Fold

Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

The short version

  • Sketch Fold doesn't collect any information about you. We have no servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your drawings, paintings, progress and word lists stay on your device.
  • Something leaves your device only when you choose to share it, save it, or use one of Apple's own features, such as buying something.

For kids

Everything you draw stays on your phone. We never see your drawings, your name or your words. If you want to share a picture or buy something, ask a grown-up first.

Who we are

Sketch Fold is an app made by [developer name] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use Sketch Fold on iPhone. If you have a question, see Contact us.

Information we collect

None. Sketch Fold does not collect, store on a server, sell, rent or share personal information. We can't see what you draw or type, and we don't know who you are.

The app doesn't connect to any server of ours, and it contains no code from other companies (no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools).

What stays on your device

To work, Sketch Fold keeps some things in its own private storage on your device. Nobody else, including us, can see them.

WhatWhy
Your current sketch and captionSo you can pick up where you left off
Saved pieces in your gallery (sketch, painting, title)So you can look at them, replay them and share them later
Your level, XP, streak and unlocked stylesTo keep track of your progress
Today's drawing ideaSo the idea stays the same all day
Your own Fold & Guess word listsSo you can play with your words again
Your chosen style, brush and inkSo the app remembers your settings

Player names you type for Fold & Guess are used only during the game. Replay videos you make are kept in temporary storage until you share them or your device clears them.

If you back up your device with iCloud Backup or to a computer, this app storage may be included in that backup. Apple handles those backups under Apple's Privacy Policy.

On-device AI

Sketch Fold uses Apple's on-device features to turn your sketch into a painting: Image Playground, Apple Intelligence's language model and Apple's image recognition. These run on your device. Sketch Fold doesn't send your drawings or words anywhere to do this. On devices without Apple Intelligence, the app uses its own built-in painter, which also runs entirely on your device.

Apple Intelligence is an Apple feature, covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

When something leaves your device

Only when you choose:

  • Share. When you tap Share, Apple's share sheet opens and you choose where your picture or video goes (for example Messages, AirDrop or Mail). What happens next is up to that app or person.
  • Save to Photos. If you save a picture or video, it goes into your Photos library. Sketch Fold can only add to your library; it can't see your photos.
  • Refine in Image Playground. This opens Apple's Image Playground. Apple controls what happens there. If you choose a style there that comes from another company (for example ChatGPT, if you've turned it on in Settings), Apple sends your picture to that company under Apple's and that company's terms, not ours.

Purchases

Sketch Fold offers a few optional extras (art styles, inks, a word pack and a tip). Apple handles every purchase through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. A grown-up has to answer a question before any purchase can start. With Family Sharing, Ask to Buy also works.

Apple gives developers sales reports that don't identify you. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Crash reports and app statistics from Apple

If you've turned on Share With App Developers in your device's Settings (Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us. Apple removes information that identifies you before we see them, and we use them only to fix problems. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

Safety checks

To keep things friendly, Sketch Fold checks captions, names and words against a list of words that aren't allowed, and, when your device's Sensitive Content Warning or Communication Safety is on, uses Apple's on-device check to block nude images. All of this happens on your device. Nothing is reported to us or anyone else.

Children

Sketch Fold is made to be safe and fun for all ages, including children. Because we don't collect personal information from anyone, we don't collect personal information from children, including children under 13 in the United States or under 16 in the European Union and United Kingdom. Parents don't need to give us anything for their child to use the app.

Purchases need a grown-up, and the app has no chat, no public posting and no way to contact other people.

Your choices and your data

  • Delete a saved piece from the gallery with its Delete button.
  • Remove your own words, or clear the whole list, in the word list editor.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device.

Because we don't hold any of your data, there's nothing for us to give you a copy of, correct or delete. If you live somewhere with privacy laws that give you rights over your data (such as the GDPR in Europe or the UAE's data protection law), you can still contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Sketch Fold handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app. If a change ever meant collecting data, we'd say so in the app first.
