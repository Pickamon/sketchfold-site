Support

Draw on one half of your phone and watch it turn into a painting on the other. Here's help with the most common questions.

Need help?

Email [contact email]. Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. We reply within 7 days.

Reporting a picture: if you tapped Remove and Tell the Developer, the picture is already gone from the device. Email us what it showed, any words that were typed, the style, and roughly when it happened.

Drawing and painting

How does my sketch become a painting?

Draw on the canvas and stop for a moment. Sketch Fold paints your sketch in the style you picked. Type a few words in the caption bar if you want to tell it what you drew. Tap Remix for a new version.

Why does it say "Ink Engine · works offline"?

The Ink Engine is Sketch Fold's own painter, and it works offline. On iOS 27 (including the iPhone Duo), Apple no longer lets apps paint with Image Playground, so the Ink Engine paints every picture. With Apple Intelligence on, Sketch Fold still uses it to write each painting's title, and Refine can repaint a picture in Apple's Image Playground. On iOS 26, turning on Apple Intelligence in Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri lets Image Playground paint; until it finishes downloading, or on devices without Apple Intelligence, the Ink Engine paints.

Some words aren't allowed. Why?

Sketch Fold is made for all ages, so it checks captions, player names and word lists for words that aren't friendly. Try different words. The check happens on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

How do I save a piece?

Tap Save, or just fold your phone shut, like closing a sketchbook. Saved pieces are in your gallery, where you can watch a replay of how they were made and share them.

How do I delete a piece?

Open the gallery, pick the piece and tap Delete. Sketch Fold asks first, because a deleted piece can't be brought back.

Do I need a folding phone?

No. Sketch Fold is made for the iPhone Duo, where the layout follows the hinge, but every action also has a button, so it works on any iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Hinge features need iOS 27.1 or later.

Fold & Guess

How do I play?
  • Tap Play, add 2 to 6 players, and tap Start Game.
  • With the phone folded shut, the artist sees a secret word on the outer screen.
  • Unfold to start the 60-second round. The artist draws, everyone else shouts guesses, and the AI tries to guess too.
  • Fold shut and pass the phone to the next artist.

You can also play solo against the AI, or use your own word list.

Purchases

What can I buy?

Optional extras only: two art styles, a set of inks, an Adventure word pack, and a tip for the developer. Levels, XP and safety checks can't be bought or skipped. A grown-up has to answer a question before any purchase.

I bought something but it isn't showing. What do I do?

Tap the round level badge at the top of the screen, scroll to the bottom and tap Shop, then scroll to the bottom of the shop and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

It says "Waiting for approval".

Ask to Buy is on for your account. The extra unlocks as soon as the family organizer approves it.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Sketch Fold doesn't collect any information about you. Your drawings stay on your device unless you share them or tap Refine, which on iOS 27 sends the sketch to Apple to finish it. Read the Privacy Policy.
